# 📊 Retail Sales & Profit Analysis

> **Data Analyst Project | Excel | Business Intelligence | Data Visualization**

A complete **Retail Sales & Profit Analysis** project focused on cleaning raw sales data, performing business analysis, identifying key trends, and building an interactive Excel dashboard.

This project was completed as part of my **Data Analyst Internship** under the mentorship of **Tarun Kumar**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to work with a real-world retail sales dataset and apply the complete data analytics workflow:

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Data Analysis → Business Insights → Dashboard**

The project focuses on understanding sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regional performance, and monthly sales trends.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the analysis:

* **Total Sales:** ₹1,49,15,600.82
* **Total Profit:** ₹15,21,767.98
* **Total Distinct Orders:** 5,496
* **Overall Profit Margin:** 10.2%

### Major Findings

* **Technology** was the top-performing product category.
* **Office Supplies** showed comparatively lower performance.
* **West** generated the highest sales among the regions.
* **Corporate** was the most profitable customer segment.
* **Global Troy Executive Leather Low-Back Tilter** was the top-selling product by revenue.
* Monthly sales showed noticeable variation, with **December 2009** recording the highest sales and **June 2011** the lowest.
